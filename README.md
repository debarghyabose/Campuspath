## 🌐 Project Overview

CampusPath 3D is an academic project developed as part of the **Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA) coursework under the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Institute of Engineering & Management (IEM), Kolkata.**

The project aims to demonstrate how fundamental computer science concepts, particularly graph theory, data structures, and shortest-path algorithms, can be applied to solve a practical real-world problem: university campus navigation.

The application creates an interactive three-dimensional representation of the IEM campus and models its buildings and pedestrian pathways as a weighted graph.
